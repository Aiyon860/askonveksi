# Brag Plan: Askonveksi

## What is this app?
Askonveksi is the operational system and public site for a Semarang workshop that makes custom uniforms and apparel: a WhatsApp inbox, a CRM pipeline, and a production board from cut to done.

## The angle
A quiet control-room film. One fictional order walks the real path the app already has: Kotak Masuk, then Pipeline, then Produksi. The punchline is the site's own line, "Dari Desain / Hingga Siap Pakai."

The chat text and the "120 pcs" quantity are stand-ins, not a real customer, order, or phone number. Stage names, module names, and the outro lines are taken from the product.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A Kotak Masuk bubble lands on the work canvas: "Butuh 120 seragam dinas." The line "Satu chat." is framing, not a product claim.

## Key moments (the middle)
- One card, "Seragam Dinas," steps Follow Up → Negosiasi → Deal.
- The same card steps Potong → Jahit → QC → Selesai on the Non-Jersey board. Those are real stage names; the video shows four of them, not the full route.
- At the strong cue, the Selesai column takes the success tint.

## Outro / punchline
Askonveksi. Then the site overlay, held long enough to read: "Dari Desain" / "Hingga Siap Pakai." Eyebrow: "Konveksi di Semarang."

## User flow worth showing
Entry: a message in Kotak Masuk. Key action: the peluang card reaches Deal. Result: the work order sits in Selesai.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet factory control room
- Interpretation: few scenes, soft crossfades, one idea at a time, no joke and no startup adjectives.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#eff3f7` (kanvas operate, `oklch(0.962 0.007 256)`)
- Accent: `#0053c2` (kontrol utama, `oklch(0.47 0.19 258)`) — one accent per group, not a field of blue
- Text: `#0a0a0a` (`oklch(0.145 0 0)`)
- Cards: `#ffffff` on the canvas, radius 10px, line `#e5e5e5`
- Success: surface `#e0fce5`, ink `#104020`
- Display font: Inter
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the production board, one white card moving across real column labels

## Share copy (draft)
Askonveksi. Konveksi di Semarang untuk seragam dan apparel custom. Dari desain hingga siap pakai.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed under sparse UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (trimmed and faded into `composition/assets/music/bed.mp3`)
- Music treatment: low under the voice of the UI, fade in 0.45s, fade out over the last 1.4s. No narration.
- Music cue guidance: preset `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.md`, about 110 BPM. Strong cues to lock: 15.82s (Selesai tint), 18.01s ("Hingga Siap Pakai"). Beat grid for card steps uses every other beat so labels stay readable: 6.82, 8.22, 11.47, 12.56, 13.64.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; bass energy only breathes a blue glow behind the canvas. No bars, notes, or strobe.
- SFX posture: sparse, low high-frequency risk, one sound per real motion.
- Audio-coupled moments: bubble arrival, each card step, Selesai tint, wordmark.
- Restraint rule: do not tick every beat, and do not cover the bed with impacts.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Kotak Masuk — 4.64s
Kicker "Kotak Masuk." Framing line "Satu chat." A white bubble reads "Butuh 120 seragam dinas."
Sequential/interaction: the bubble arrives after the line, as one incoming message.
Audio intent: a small click as the bubble lands, bed already in.
Audio-coupled idea: bubble entrance with a soft click.
Music: low warm bed.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Pipeline — 5.19s
Page title "Pipeline." Columns Follow Up, Negosiasi, Deal. The card "Seragam Dinas / 120 pcs" starts in Follow Up, steps to Negosiasi, then Deal, and holds in Deal.
Sequential/interaction: two steps, each held. Column labels stay on screen the whole scene.
Audio intent: a short rollover on each step.
Audio-coupled idea: card step on the beat grid (6.82s, 8.22s).
Music: same bed.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Produksi — 6.55s
Page title "Produksi." Columns Potong, Jahit, QC, Selesai. Kicker on the card: "Non-Jersey." The card steps across and rests in Selesai. At 15.82s the Selesai column takes the success surface.
Sequential/interaction: three steps, then the success tint.
Audio intent: rollover per step, one soft impact on the tint.
Audio-coupled idea: steps on 11.47s, 12.56s, 13.64s; tint beat-locked to 15.82s.
Music: same bed, cue used only for the tint.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Siap pakai — 4.62s
"Konveksi di Semarang." Wordmark "Askonveksi." Then "Dari Desain" and "Hingga Siap Pakai," the second line beat-locked to 18.01s. Hold, then a short fade on the canvas.
Sequential/interaction: the four lines arrive one after another and stay until the fade.
Audio intent: one soft tone on the wordmark, then the bed fades.
Audio-coupled idea: wordmark tone; the 18.01s line is the locked reveal.
Music: bed fades out.
Transition mood: fade on the final scene only.

**Music mood for this video:** warm, restrained, about 110 BPM.
**Audio summary:** A low bed, a click for the message, rollovers for the card, one impact when Selesai lands, one tone for the name, then the bed leaves.
